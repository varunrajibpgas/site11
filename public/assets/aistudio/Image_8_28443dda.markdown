## Brand & Style

This design system elevates industrial energy infrastructure into a sophisticated, modern, and trustworthy digital experience. Synthesizing elements of **Modern Corporate** reliability and **High-Contrast Clean Tech**, it projects environmental responsibility, infrastructural mass, and fiscal stability. 

The audience comprises commercial fuel consumers, prospective energy dealership franchisees, institutional partners, and industrial clients seeking high-standard supply chain integrity. 

Key visual principles:
- **Commanding Presence**: Deep forest and midnight greens anchor heroes, primary dashboards, and metric highlights.
- **Luminescent Clarity**: Vibrant parrot green and fresh lime serve as precision beacons for primary conversion points, verified status indicators, and energetic accents.
- **Air and Tactility**: High contrast between deep saturated zones and generous warm off-white content canvases ensures long-form reading comfort without losing authoritative weight.

## Colors

The palette establishes an alternation between monumental dark surfaces and pristine light content areas.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#14532D`)**: The bedrock brand green. Applied to core badges, prominent structural cards, and high-impact identity marks.
- **Secondary (`#65C466`)**: Energetic parrot green. Drives active interactive states, key buttons on dark backdrops, progress bars, and glowing structural dividers.
- **Tertiary (`#B7F36B`)**: Fresh lime highlight. Reserved for precision accents, badges indicating verification (e.g., active bottling plants, ISO certification), and high-priority metrics.
- **Neutral (`#A7B2AA`)**: Subdued industrial gray with an organic green tint. Utilized for meta labels, borders, inactive indicators, and secondary typography.

### Contextual Canvas Shifts
- **Dark Mode Zones (`#092B1D`, `#10251B`)**: Applied across hero headers, key performance indicator (KPI) boards, and global footers. Surfaces use subtle emerald radial glows to simulate ambient backlighting.
- **Light Field Zones (`#F7F8F3`, `#FFFFFF`)**: Applied to complex data tables, documentation, franchise inquiry forms, and dense operational grids to maintain legibility and professional clarity.

## Typography

The type system balances the confident, geometric architecture of **Plus Jakarta Sans** with the utilitarian precision of **Inter**.

- **Display & Headings**: Rendered in Plus Jakarta Sans. Tight negative tracking gives large titles a solid, mechanical authority appropriate for heavy infrastructure and corporate scale.
- **Body & Data**: Rendered in Inter. Generous x-height and clear apertures guarantee effortless comprehension across high-density logistics tables, compliance manifests, and franchise prospectuses.
- **Numerical Statistics**: Large stat figures (e.g., `300+ Dealers`, `1,00,000+ Customers`) utilize `stat-metric` with Plus Jakarta Sans to maximize visual impact and project operational trust.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a structured **12-column responsive fluid grid** with generous spatial boundaries to maintain an executive feel.

### Spatial Architecture
- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns, 24px gutters, max-width bounded at 1320px with centered margin pools. Section breaks scale between `space-xl` and `space-2xl` to establish distinctive regional hierarchy between dark thematic banners and light content grids.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8 columns, 20px gutters, 32px canvas margins. Multi-metric stat rows compress from 4-columns to a 2x2 grid.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, 16px gutters, 20px safe outer margins. Metric badges, franchise forms, and facility cards collapse to full-width structural modules.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **ambient luminescence** paired with **low-contrast architectural outlines**, avoiding harsh, artificial drop shadows.

### Elevation Levels
1. **Level 0 (Flat Ground)**:
   - Dark zones: `#092B1D` or `#10251B`.
   - Light zones: `#F7F8F3`.
2. **Level 1 (Card & Module Layer)**:
   - Dark zones: Solid `#10251B` background with a crisp border of `rgba(101, 196, 102, 0.12)`.
   - Light zones: `#FFFFFF` card with a structural border of `rgba(9, 43, 29, 0.06)` and an ambient shadow `0 8px 24px rgba(9, 43, 29, 0.04)`.
3. **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Active State)**:
   - Dark zones: Border illuminated to `rgba(101, 196, 102, 0.35)` with an inner emerald glow and shadow `0 12px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`.
   - Light zones: Elevated card with shadow `0 16px 36px rgba(9, 43, 29, 0.08)`.
4. **Level 3 (Modal / Energy Dialogs)**:
   - Centered elevated planes with backdrop filters (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) and a diffused green accent ring: `box-shadow: 0 24px 64px rgba(9, 43, 29, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The system implements a **Rounded (Level 2)** shape standard:
- Standard elements (form fields, interactive buttons, tag chips) use `0.5rem` (`8px`) radii, providing a modern, approachable edge while preserving corporate precision.
- Structural cards and data panels scale to `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) to establish friendly frames for dense metric content.
- Hero imagery, large banners, and modal overlays adopt `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`).
- Pure pill shapes are strictly preserved for status badges (e.g., active pipeline health, franchise tier status).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (High-Energy)**: `#65C466` background with `#092B1D` bold text. On hover, shifts to `#B7F36B` accompanied by a soft green ambient halo (`0 0 20px rgba(101, 196, 102, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary (Dark Contrast)**: Deep forest green (`#14532D`) surface with `#FFFFFF` text and a subtle `#65C466` border.
- **Tertiary (Ghost / Outline)**: Transparent background with `rgba(167, 178, 170, 0.4)` border, transitioning to fully opaque parrot-green text on hover.

### Metrics & Stat Counters
- Designed specifically for institutional credibility counters (`300+ Dealers`, `1,00,000+ Customers`, `5 Bottling Plants`).
- Metrics sit on elevated surfaces with an integrated top border accent strip in `#65C466` or `#B7F36B`. Numbers are displayed in `stat-metric` styling, accompanied by an uppercase `label-sm` subtitle in `#A7B2AA`.

### Cards & Industrial Feature Grids
- **Network / Franchise Cards**: Base color `#FFFFFF` inside light sections, bordered with `rgba(9, 43, 29, 0.08)`. Icons sit within a soft-tinted circle of `rgba(101, 196, 102, 0.15)` utilizing `#14532D` iconography.
- **Dark Feature Pods**: Enclosed in `#10251B`, flanked by fine 1px neon parrot-green edge lines and featuring micro badges for tracking operational stats.

### Forms & Inquiry Inputs
- Designed for dealer franchise registrations and lead captures.
- Inputs feature a tall touch target (48px height), background `#FFFFFF` (or `rgba(255,255,255,0.05)` on dark panels), with an inactive border of `#A7B2AA`. Focus transitions the border to `#65C466` with a delicate 3px glow (`rgba(101, 196, 102, 0.2)`).

### Badges & Verification Chips
- Compact pills utilizing `label-sm`. For operational verification, background is `rgba(183, 243, 107, 0.15)` with `#092B1D` or `#B7F36B` text and a leading glowing dot indicator.
